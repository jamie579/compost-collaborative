Jamie B. Smith

(he/him)

PhD, MA, MSc, BSc (Hons), RN · Berlin and the UK

ORCID Scholar Personal website Charité profile Napier research record Wikidata

Jamie B Smith is a white, cis, queer newly middle class man. He brings nursing practice, institutional ethnography and critical posthuman philosophy to the Compost Collaborative’s shared work on care, power and more-than-human relations.

With fellow members, he co-authored Critical Posthuman Nursing Care and Mattering: Per/forming nursing philosophy in the Chthulucene. For his wider research, publications, consultancy and speaking, visit his personal website.
