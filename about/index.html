About us

We imagine otherwise, with creativity as critique.

We are an international collective of nurses, artists, critical theorists and anti-fascists. Nursing brings us together; it does not set the limits of what we can make or question.

What grows here

We work with critical posthumanism and feminist new materialism to ask how care takes shape between people, institutions, technologies and other living beings. Queer, feminist, abolitionist and anti-fascist thought runs through our writing, conversations and creative work.

Our projects include books, journal collaborations, movement, film and experiments in noticing. The collective bibliography gathers work by members; activities records the places where we meet, speak and make things together.

Where we stand

We bring different histories and privileges to this work. Our position statement is the place where we name those conditions and their limits. It remains open to change as the collective changes.
