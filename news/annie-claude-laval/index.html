Annie-Claude joins the professoriate

Appointment announced: 27 August 2025

Université Laval welcomed Annie-Claude Laurin as a professeure assistante in its Faculté des sciences infirmières on 27 August 2025.

The announcement describes her intensive-care nursing experience and her research on ethics, epistemic injustice and nurses’ moral agency, including the political and philosophical dimensions of advanced nursing practice.
