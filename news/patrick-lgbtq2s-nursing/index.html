Funding for LGBTQ2S+ nursing research

Funding announced: 5 February 2025

On 5 February 2025, Université Laval announced a grant of CAD $14,000 from the Fondation de l’Institut universitaire de cardiologie et de pneumologie de Québec for Patrick Martin’s research.

The project explores the experiences of LGBTQ2S+ nurses caring for LGBTQ2S+ patients. It brings questions of care, sexuality and gender into nursing research.

Read Laval’s funding announcement, or meet Patrick and find his writing on care ethics and nurse resistance.
