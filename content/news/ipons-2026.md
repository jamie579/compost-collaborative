The 28th International Nursing Philosophy Conference met at UC Irvine and online on 22–24 July 2026. Its theme was *Listening and Speaking Otherwise: Encounters at the Edge of Nursing and Philosophy*. Jess co-chaired with Miriam Bender.

Seven members were represented on the programme. Jane facilitated *Nursing the Borderlands*, with Annie-Claude, Brandon and Eva. Jamie and Jess held the invited conversation *Camp and the Politics of Seriousness in Nursing and Health Philosophy*. Jamie also facilitated *Camp, Professionalism, and Performative Critique*, which included *Abstractification in nursing education*, co-authored by Tue-Chieu Mai and Patrick. Jamie joined *Thinking the Event of Nursing*. Jess facilitated *Nursing Crip Futures*.
