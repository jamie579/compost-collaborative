---
title: "Funding for LGBTQ2S+ nursing research"
description: "Patrick Martin's research on LGBTQ2S+ nurses' experiences of caring for LGBTQ2S+ patients received Fondation de l’IUCPQ funding in February 2025."
---

On 5 February 2025, Université Laval announced a grant of CAD $14,000 from the Fondation de l'Institut universitaire de cardiologie et de pneumologie de Québec for Patrick Martin's research.

The project explores the experiences of LGBTQ2S+ nurses caring for LGBTQ2S+ patients. It brings questions of care, sexuality and gender into nursing research.

[Read Laval's funding announcement](https://www.fsi.ulaval.ca/actualites/nouvelle-subvention-pour-patrick-martin-de-la-fondation-de-liucpq), or [meet Patrick](../../people/patrick/) and find his writing on care ethics and nurse resistance.
