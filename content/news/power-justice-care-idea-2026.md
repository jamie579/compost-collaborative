Annie-Claude Laurin and Anne-Marie Gagné-Julien are named as co-hosts of IDÉA’s autumn 2026 series, [Relations de pouvoir, justice et soins de santé](https://www.idea.ulaval.ca/actualites/une-nouvelle-serie-de-conferences-en-ethique-et-sante-lautomne-2026). The series examines power, marginalisation and professional agency in healthcare institutions.

As of 20 November 2026, the 22 October session was cancelled, and Phoebe Friesen’s session, originally scheduled for 19 November, has been [postponed to winter 2027](https://www.idea.ulaval.ca/evenements/reporte-lhiver-2027-conference-midi-anesthetized-bodies-teaching-tools-and-hidden-moral-curriculum).
