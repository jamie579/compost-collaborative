Jamie B Smith is a white, cis, queer newly middle class man. He brings nursing practice, institutional ethnography and critical posthuman philosophy to the Compost Collaborative’s shared work on care, power and more-than-human relations.

With fellow members, he co-authored *Critical Posthuman Nursing Care* and *Mattering: Per/forming nursing philosophy in the Chthulucene*. For his wider research, publications, consultancy and speaking, visit his [personal website](https://jamiebriansmith.com/).
