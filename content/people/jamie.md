Jamie B Smith is a white, cis, queer newly middle class man. He brings nursing practice, institutional ethnography and critical posthuman philosophy to the Compost Collaborative’s shared work on care, power and more-than-human relations.

With fellow members, he co-authored *Critical Posthuman Nursing Care* and *Mattering: Per/forming nursing philosophy in the Chthulucene*. Recent collaborations include *Making Care Perceptible: Nursing/Intuitive Movement/Posthuman Care* (2025), *An Ethics of Artificial Intelligence for Nursing* (2025), and *Beyond Heroic Individualism* (2026). For his wider research, publications, consultancy and speaking, visit his [personal website](https://jamiebriansmith.com/).
