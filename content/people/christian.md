Christian Tedjasukmana (he/him) RN, BSN, MFA is a nurse, artist, and designer based in the USA. His photography brings hospital waste into view, while his design work includes visual identities for nursing communities. His public work includes the mural *The Assimilation of Emmanuel*, part of Burlington City Arts' [4 x 8 Mural Project](https://www.burlingtoncityarts.org/4-x-8-mural-project-legacy) on race and belonging.

## Art and design

- [*Myopia: Harming in Order to Heal*](https://nursingfuturities.org/2022/01/05/emmanuel-christian-tedjasukmana-rn-bsn-mfa-he-him-his/) is a photographic body of work about hospital waste. His hospital-waste photography is discussed and reproduced in [*Notes on [post]human nursing*](https://doi.org/10.1111/nin.12562).
- Christian designed the [Nursing Theory Conference logo](https://nursingtheoryconference.com/2019/11/18/meet-the-nursing-theory-conference-logo/), introduced in 2019. In August 2026, the organisers [announced that Nursology Forums would retain his design](https://nursingtheoryconference.com/2026/08/04/announcing-the-new-nursology-forums-the-next-bold-chapter-to-honor-and-replace-the-virtual-nursing-theory-week/).
- He created the Nursing Mutual Aid illustration credited in [Rae Walker’s 2020 essay on institutional racism in nursing education](https://rachelkwalker.medium.com/dear-nursing-education-if-we-cant-even-acknowledge-the-institutional-racism-apparent-in-our-eb66957fcb8f).
