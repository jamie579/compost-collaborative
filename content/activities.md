## Medical (Post) Humanities Conference

Jamie explored the issues with person-centred care, considering who the 'person' is in this context. He argued that a person-centred approach to medicine assumes the person to be a rational agent without vulnerabilities or contingencies, and additionally that evidence-based approaches rely further on the patients' body fitting normative categories.

## What nursing chooses not to know: practices of epistemic silence/silencing

Jess led an invited panel at the International Philosophy of Nursing Conference 2022 discussing how nurses silence certain knowledges while privileging others. Moderated by Jessica Dillard-Wright and featuring Lucinda Canty, Ismalia De Sousa, Janice Gullick, Amélie Perron, and Claire Valderama-Wallace.
