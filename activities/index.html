2026

IPONS 28th International Nursing Philosophy Conference — UC Irvine, 22–24 July 2026. Seven of us on one programme, “Listening and Speaking Otherwise: Encounters at the Edge of Nursing and Philosophy”. Jess co-chaired the conference. The collective’s symposium Nursing the Borderlands brought together Jane (facilitating), Annie-Claude, Brandon and Eva. Jamie and Jess held the invited conversation Camp and the Politics of Seriousness in Nursing and Health Philosophy — what happens when nursing stops being polite and starts getting real — and Jamie facilitated the panel Camp, Professionalism, and Performative Critique (with Patrick presenting) and joined the invited conversation Thinking the Event of Nursing. Jess also facilitated the symposium Nursing Crip Futures. Conference announcement →

“Rumor Without Recourse: The Lavender Scare Comes to Teacher’s College” — Jess presented her queer nursing history research at the UMass Elaine Marieb College of Nursing speaker series, March 2026.

From the archive: 2022

Medical (Post) Humanities Conference — Jamie explored the issues with person-centred care, considering who the ‘person’ is in this context: a person-centred approach to medicine assumes the person to be a rational agent without vulnerabilities or contingencies, and evidence-based approaches rely further on the patient’s body fitting normative categories.

What nursing chooses not to know: practices of epistemic silence/silencing — Jess led an invited panel at the International Philosophy of Nursing Conference 2022, featuring Lucinda Canty, Ismalia De Sousa, Janice Gullick, Amélie Perron, and Claire Valderama-Wallace. Watch →

Radical Philosophy Hour: Nursing in the time of COVID — the collective’s talk on new materialist and posthumanist perspectives (necropolitics of ECMO), Radical Philosophy Association, April 2022. Watch →
